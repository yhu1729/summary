### Summary

This study numerically examines ponderomotive density structuring by broadband kinetic Alfvén-wave turbulence under plasma-sheet-boundary-layer conditions. A two-dimensional pseudospectral solver advances coupled modified nonlinear Schrödinger and magnetosonic equations on a $256\times256$ grid with $\beta\sim0.1$--$0.3$. The undamped calculation conserves energy within $0.085\%$ through normalized time 40, while a scalar Landau-damping run loses about $4\%$ of magnetic energy. The reported nonlinearity parameter remains near $0.25$, indicating a sub-Alfvénic regime. Magnetic filaments and co-located density cavities emerge within several wave periods; density perturbations reach $|\delta n/n_0|\sim0.3$--$0.5$, although the domain-wide intensity--density correlation is only $-0.022$. Spectra transition rapidly from injection to dissipation without an extended inertial range, which the authors associate with moderate magnetic Reynolds number. Interpretation is limited by the two-dimensional fluid-envelope model, parallel-only acoustic response, scalar damping, fixed ion temperature, and absence of self-consistent wave--particle resonances.

### Contributions

1. Simulated broadband, rather than single-packet, kinetic Alfvén-wave forcing with a coupled MNLS--magnetosonic model.
2. Documented early formation and persistence of intermittent magnetic filaments and density cavities at ion-scale widths.
3. Quantified numerical conservation, damping losses, and sub-Alfvénic nonlinearity over the full run.
4. Reported a compressed injection-to-dissipation spectrum and interpreted it through moderate-Reynolds-number scale separation.
5. Compared the simulated structures with plasma-sheet observations while explicitly identifying missing three-dimensional and kinetic physics.
