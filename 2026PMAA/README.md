## Claude (September 2026)

### Summary

Laser wakefield acceleration (LWFA) is limited by dephasing: trapped electrons outrun the accelerating phase of a wake moving at the driver's subluminal group velocity. As background, flying-focus pulses drive luminal wakes but bring a varying spot size, long duration, or large plasma volume. The authors build the driver in a preformed parabolic plasma waveguide from $N$ radial modes with frequencies $\omega_q=\omega_0+q\Omega$, so that the modal interference peak travels at $c$ with constant spot size and ultrashort duration while the envelope moves at the group velocity. Setting the peak duration to $\pi/\omega_{p0}$, with $\omega_{p0}$ the on-axis plasma frequency, fixes the matched spot size (the waveguide's lowest-mode width); matching the recurrence period $2\pi/\Omega$ to the envelope duration fixes the stage length at $N/\sigma$ dephasing lengths at equal density, with $\sigma$ between 0.88 and 1. Linear-regime scaling laws compare energy gain, stage length, pulse energy, and density with standard LWFA. Quasi-3D particle-in-cell simulations with ten modes at $6\times10^{18}$ cm$^{-3}$ give an 11-fold longer stage and energy gain, in close agreement with scaling values of 660 versus 58 MeV at normalized amplitude $a_0=0.5$. Stated costs are broader bandwidth, system complexity, larger matched spots, and self-focusing and depletion at $a_0\sim0.75$.

### Contributions

1. Showed that imposing $a_{oa}(z+v_a\mathcal T,t+\mathcal T)=a_{oa}(z,t)e^{i\eta\mathcal T}$ on the on-axis potential cannot be met by one broadband spectrum across the dispersion relations $k_q(\omega)=c^{-1}[\omega^2-\omega_{p0}^2-4(1+2q)c^2/w^2]^{1/2}$ but is met by one frequency per mode, $\omega_q=\omega_0+(k_q(\omega_q)-\kappa_0)v_a$, which for $v_a=c$ is $\omega_q=\omega_0+4c^2q/[(\omega_0-c\kappa_0)w^2]$ and for $(\omega_{p0}w/2c)^2\gg1$ has spacing $\Omega=2\omega_0(2c/\omega_{p0}w)^2$, and derived the on-axis intensity $N^{-2}|\alpha(t-z/v_g)|^2\sin^2[\frac12N\Omega(t-z/c)]/\sin^2[\frac12\Omega(t-z/c)]$, whose peaks of FWHM $\tau=(\sigma\pi/N\omega_0)(\omega_{p0}w/2c)^2$ cross an envelope of duration $T$ in $L_D=cv_gT/(c-v_g)\approx2(\omega_0/\omega_{p0})^2cT$.
2. Derived from $\tau=\pi/\omega_{p0}$ the matched spot size $w=(2c/\omega_{p0})(\omega_0/\omega_{p0})^{1/2}(N/\sigma)^{1/2}$ and the FWHM spot $w_F=(2c/\omega_{p0})(\omega_0/\omega_{p0})^{1/2}(2\ln2/\sigma)^{1/2}$, smaller than $w$ for $N>1$, that sets the wake width, and from $T_R\simeq T$ the stage length $L_D=(2\pi c/\omega_{p0})(\omega_0/\omega_{p0})^2N/\sigma$, which with the wake field $e|E_z|/mc=(\pi/8)\omega_{p0}a_0^2$ give $\Delta\gamma_D=(\pi^2/4)(N/\sigma)(\omega_0/\omega_{pD})^2a_0^2$ and, for a flattop envelope, the pulse energy $U_D/mc^2=(\pi cN/4\sigma^2r_e\omega_0)(\omega_0/\omega_{pD})^4a_0^2$, both equal to the standard values for $N=\sigma=1$ at equal density.
3. Compared DLWFA with a standard pulse of equal normalized FWHM spot, $\omega_{pD}w_{FD}=\omega_{pS}w_{FS}$: at fixed density the gain, stage-length, and pulse-energy ratios all equal $N/\sigma$ with $w_D=N^{1/2}w_S$; at fixed stage length the gain ratio is $(N/\sigma)^{1/3}$ with $U_D=U_S$, $w_D=(\sigma^2N)^{1/6}w_S$, and $n_D=(N/\sigma)^{2/3}n_S$; at fixed gain $L_D/L_S=U_D/U_S=(\sigma/N)^{1/2}$ with $w_D=\sigma^{1/2}w_S$ and $n_D=(N/\sigma)n_S$; and at equal density $P/P_{cr}=\omega_{pD}^2w_D^2a_0^2/(32c^2N)$ equals the standard value because $w_{FD}=w_{FS}$.
4. Simulated with OSIRIS, using azimuthal modes 0 and 1, the designs with $\lambda_0=1$ µm, $\tau=23$ fs, $w_F=20$ µm, $w=17$ and 54 µm, $\Lambda=(\lambda_0/\omega_0)\Omega=13$ nm, $L_d=2.5$ mm, and $L_D=2.8$ cm, in a $178\times323$ µm window of $4980\times1490$ cells for 109 ps with a 258-fs flattop envelope between 36-fs ramps, finding the first recurrence of the luminal peak near $\omega_{p0}t\approx10^4$, temporal pulse splitting that settles into a steady state at $a_0=0.5$, and at $a_0=0.75$ self-focusing, depletion, redshifting, and compression that slow the wake until a burst dislocates its phase, repeatedly; within the FWHM spot both pulses and wakes are nearly identical, while outside it modal interference gives the wake multiple transverse periods.
5. Projected for $a_0=0.5$ a 100-GeV accelerator of five ten-mode DLWFA stages at $2\times10^{17}$ cm$^{-3}$ with $L_D=4.7$ m and matched spot 690 µm, against ten 10-GeV standard stages at $3.5\times10^{16}$ cm$^{-3}$ with $L_d=5.7$ m, with 2.4 times less total pulse energy, estimated the waveguide plasma volume $V\propto\pi Nw^2L=\sigma(\omega_{p0}/\omega_0)^3L^3/\pi$ against $\pi L^3/(12f_\#^2)$ for the full-aperture axiparabola cone, and showed that luminal peaks resonantly driving a multi-pulse wake with $\tau=\pi\sigma/\omega_{p0}$ and $T_R=2\pi\ell/\omega_{p0}$ need $N=2\ell$ modes.

(18) with $N=\sigma=1$ at $n_S$ gives about 760 µm; neither yields 160 µm, whereas the quoted $w_D=690$ µm and pulse-energy factor 2.4 follow from Eqs. (18), (24), and (25); unresolved.

(A1), and hence Eq. (14), keeps $\exp[-iq\Omega(t-z/c)]$ and places the delay in the envelope $\alpha(t)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\tilde\alpha(\omega)e^{-i\omega(t-t_d)}d\omega$, which requires the phase $(\omega-\omega_q)t_d$ rather than $q\Omega t_d$; unresolved.
