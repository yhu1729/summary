### Summary

This paper computationally compares best $L^2$ and $L^\infty$ rational approximations of analytic functions on the unit disk. Although existence and characterization theory for these approximation problems is classical, practical computation has been much less developed. For the $L^\infty$ problem, the authors use the AAA-Lawson method; for the $L^2$ problem, they formulate computations through TF-IRKA and a barycentric representation. The paper derives Hermite interpolation conditions for exact barycentric rational functions and shows how Loewner or barycentric linear systems can enforce the required interpolation and derivative constraints. Numerical examples compare the two norms, pole locations, approximation errors, and cases with nonunique global best approximants. The authors find no dramatic difference in approximation power between $L^2$ and $L^\infty$ rational approximants on bounded domains, while emphasizing that rational functions remain especially valuable for singularities near boundaries, nonconvex domains, and model-reduction-style problems.

4. Derived barycentric Hermite interpolation conditions using rectangular Loewner matrices.
