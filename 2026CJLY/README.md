### Summary

This paper studies stabilization of the magnetized Vlasov--Poisson system by an imposed electric field. Laplace--Fourier analysis yields a dispersion relation and a Penrose-type criterion for Bernstein modes perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field. Gaussian equilibria exhibit persistent but bounded oscillations, whereas a ring-shaped equilibrium supports the Dory--Guest--Harris instability. The authors design controls by modifying or eliminating unstable dispersion roots. One strategy chooses the external potential to cancel the self-consistent field, recovering magnetized free streaming exactly; for separable Gaussian data, non-Bernstein electric-energy modes then decay while Bernstein modes remain periodic at the gyrofrequency. A second root-placement strategy suppresses selected unstable modes without full cancellation. Particle simulations agree with the linear predictions and show stabilization persisting in tested nonlinear cases. The approach provides an analytically guided control principle, but the geometry and field are idealized; extension to drift-kinetic and gyrokinetic regimes is left open.

### Contributions

1. Derived the magnetized dispersion relation and a Bernstein-mode Penrose stability condition.
2. Characterized stable Gaussian and unstable Dory--Guest--Harris equilibria within one framework.
3. Formulated electric-field controls through unstable-root elimination and placement.
4. Proved bounded controlled energy behavior for the exact free-streaming strategy.
5. Demonstrated suppression of unstable modes in linear and tested nonlinear simulations.
