### Summary

This paper projects the flux-tube gyrokinetic equation onto Laguerre polynomials in magnetic moment and Hermite polynomials in parallel velocity. The resulting pseudo-spectral moment hierarchy smoothly connects low-dimensional gyrofluid models to conventional five-dimensional gyrokinetics as velocity resolution increases. Its formulation preserves density, momentum, energy, and free energy even at low resolution. A sparse model collision operator includes pitch-angle scattering, energy diffusion, slowing down, and classical diffusion while satisfying conservation laws and an $H$ theorem. The authors analyze closures generated by parallel streaming, magnetic drifts, and nonlinear phase mixing, emphasizing that sharp velocity-space structure can require high resolution. Linear ion-temperature-gradient and zonal-flow tests agree with GS2 when sufficiently resolved and expose where low-order gyrofluid closure fails. The approach underlies the GPU-oriented GX code, offering a small-memory, parallel representation and the possibility of adapting fidelity between gyrofluid efficiency and gyrokinetic accuracy.

### Contributions

1. Derived a Laguerre--Hermite pseudo-spectral representation of nonlinear flux-tube gyrokinetics.
2. Unified gyrofluid and gyrokinetic velocity resolutions within one moment hierarchy.
3. Preserved key particle, energy, and free-energy invariants at low spectral resolution.
4. Constructed a sparse conservative collision model satisfying an $H$ theorem.
5. Verified linear instabilities and zonal-flow dynamics while identifying closure and phase-mixing limits.
