### Summary

The paper derives a hot-electron-limit closure for the Hermite--Laguerre gyromoment hierarchy by expanding gyroaveraging kernels in $\tau=T_i/T_e\ll1$ and retaining the minimal $O(\tau)$ terms needed for consistent coupling. The resulting four-field system evolves density, parallel velocity, and parallel and perpendicular temperatures. In Z-pinch geometry it is analytically equivalent to the Ivanov fluid model, after an empirical factor aligns the Dougherty and Landau collision operators. Gyacomo benchmarks show that four gyromoments close in the asymptotic regime and reproduce published linear growth rates, nonlinear heat flux, low-collisionality dynamics, and parallel-domain transport plateaus. Extension to tokamak $s$--$\alpha$ geometry qualitatively preserves heat-flux dynamics even near $\tau=1$, but overpredicts growth and substantially weakens or removes the Dimits shift. Higher heat-flux and pressure-tensor moments are therefore essential for zonal-flow amplification and near-threshold transport suppression.

### Contributions

1. Derived a controlled hot-electron asymptotic closure directly from the gyromoment hierarchy.
2. Proved equivalence of the reduced Z-pinch equations to the Ivanov model.
3. Established that four gyromoments form a closed set for $\tau\ll1$.
4. Reproduced linear, nonlinear, collisionality, and parallel-domain Z-pinch benchmarks.
5. Demonstrated that omitted higher moments are crucial for the Dimits shift and zonal-flow regulation.
