### Summary

This Nuclear Fusion paper experimentally investigates how magnetic islands affect plasma flow and turbulence in the Wendelstein 7-X stellarator. The study examines magnetic configurations where the 5/5 island is placed at the plasma edge inside the last closed flux surface. A V-band Doppler reflectometer provides spatially resolved measurements of perpendicular plasma flow and density fluctuations. The data reveal a clear island signature in the perpendicular-flow profile. Comparing the measured flow with neoclassically driven $E\times B$ flow shows that the island contribution is strongest near island boundaries and nearly vanishes near the island O-point. The density fluctuation level is also reduced close to the O-point. By relating these observations to results from other devices and gyrokinetic simulations, the paper provides experimental evidence that island geometry can structure both flow shear and turbulence intensity in stellarator edge plasmas. The work helps connect W7-X island-divertor physics with broader magnetic-island turbulence studies.
