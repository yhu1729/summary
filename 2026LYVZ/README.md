### Summary

The paper enlarges the stellarator design space by combining poloidal omnigenity (PO), which suppresses orbit-averaged radial drift, with piecewise omnigenity (pwO), which relaxes the requirement that all magnetic-strength contours close poloidally. Within the OOPS framework, a restricted Landreman-Catto coordinate mapping “squeezes” the high-field region into locally closed, approximately parallelogram-shaped contours while preserving ordinary omnigenity in the low-field region. Boundary optimization produces nine PO-pwO configurations spanning two to five field periods and several aspect ratios. Their departures from exact omnigenity and effective ripple are below those of the W7-X high-mirror reference and close to a pure PO case. Reactor-scaled collisionless calculations lose less than $4\%$ of 3.5 MeV alpha particles over $0.2\,\mathrm{s}$ in all nine configurations. A further design develops a vacuum magnetic well of depth about $0.023$ while keeping alpha losses below $2\%$. MONKES calculations show low radial neoclassical transport; bootstrap-current coefficients vary because the zero-current pwO condition was not explicitly imposed, although some cases are comparable to W7-X. The method therefore demonstrates a controlled transport-versus-shaping trade-off, while leaving coil complexity and integrated reactor optimization for future work.

### Contributions

1. Formulated a restricted-coordinate “squeeze” mapping that combines PO and pwO within the OOPS optimization framework.
2. Generated nine optimized PO-pwO equilibria across multiple field periods and aspect ratios rather than tuning a single configuration.
3. Demonstrated low effective ripple and reactor-scaled alpha-particle losses below $4\%$ across the configuration family.
4. Produced a PO-pwO equilibrium with a vacuum magnetic well while retaining low neoclassical transport and below-$2\%$ alpha losses.
5. Quantified radial and bootstrap-current transport coefficients and clarified which configurations approach W7-X-level bootstrap behavior.
