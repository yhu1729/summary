# Publication Summary

Curated AI-assisted summaries of mathematics, physics, and scientific computing
publications.

- [LIST](LIST.md): Publication List
- [TOPIC](TOPIC.md): Publications by Topic
- [summary.pdf](summary.pdf): Combined PDF of all summaries

Each publication has its own directory, named by year and author initials,
containing a BibTeX entry and a `README.md` with a summary and the five most
significant contributions. Summaries are AI-generated and reviewed by a human;
the original publications remain authoritative.
