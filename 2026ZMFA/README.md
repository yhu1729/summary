## Claude (September 2026)

### Summary

The authors study the modulational instability of ion-acoustic waves in an unmagnetized, collisionless plasma of cold fluid ions and electrons following a Tsallis–Gurevich distribution, which combines nonextensive statistics with entropic index $q$ for free electrons and a flat-topped population adiabatically trapped in the potential well. They derive closed-form electron densities in terms of hypergeometric functions for $1/3<q<1$ and $q>1$; the small-potential expansions contain a $\Phi^{3/2}$ trapping term and reduce to the Tsallis and Gurevich limits. The reductive perturbation method gives a modified nonlinear Schrödinger equation with a $|\Phi|^{1/2}\Phi$ nonlinearity, dispersion coefficient $P$, and nonlinear coefficient $Q$. For $PQ>0$ it admits bright $\mathrm{sech}^4$ envelope solitons; for $PQ<0$ only unbounded $\sec^4$ solutions exist, so dark solitons are absent. Linear stability analysis predicts instability for envelope wavenumbers below $K_{\max}\propto\Phi_0^{1/4}$, with maximum growth rate $Q\Phi_0^{1/2}/4$. In parametric plots, smaller $q$ lowers the wave frequency and long-wavelength group velocity, trapping barely affects linear dispersion, $PQ$ stays positive for all examined parameters, and both $K_{\max}$ and the peak growth rate increase with $q$, especially for $q>1$, with the growth rate peaking at a critical carrier wavenumber. The model assumes one-dimensional, small-amplitude propagation.

### Contributions

1. Derived exact Tsallis–Gurevich electron densities for both ranges of $q$ and verified their Tsallis and Gurevich limits.
2. Obtained, via reductive perturbation, an envelope equation with a fractional $3/2$-order nonlinearity and explicit $q$-dependent coefficients for ion-acoustic wave packets.
3. Constructed traveling-wave solutions of this equation, showing that it supports bright solitons but, unlike the cubic nonlinear Schrödinger equation, no bounded dark solitons.
4. Derived the instability band $K<K_{\max}\propto\Phi_0^{1/4}$, rather than $\Phi_0^{1/2}$ as for the cubic equation, and the maximum growth rate, with the strongest instability at carrier wavenumber $K^2=(q+1)/4$.
5. Mapped how electron nonextensivity controls dispersion, group velocity, the instability window, and growth rates, finding modulational instability throughout the examined subextensive and superextensive regimes.

### Comments

- §III-B, last paragraph: the text states that "for the current problem $P/Q<0$, and the resulting soliton is of the bright type." This contradicts the same section, where bright $\mathrm{sech}^4$ solitons, Eqs. (37)–(38), require $P/Q>0$ and $P/Q<0$ gives only the unbounded solution of Eqs. (39)–(40), as well as Fig. 4, §III-D, the abstract, and the conclusion, which all report $PQ>0$. The intended statement is most likely $P/Q>0$.
