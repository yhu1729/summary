### Summary

This preprint derives and implements a self-consistent electrostatic model coupling large-amplitude, slowly varying full-$f$ drift-kinetic fields to small-amplitude, rapidly varying $\delta f$ gyrokinetic fields. Critical-balance ordering reduces the electron dynamics to a drift-reduced Braginskii model, while Hermite--Laguerre expansions represent both components of the ion distribution through gyromoments. Global simulations with Large Plasma Device parameters converge with few moments because ion--ion collisions keep the drift-kinetic distribution close to a bi-Maxwellian. At the physical collisionality, gyrokinetic fields remain subdominant and do not materially change the drift-kinetic turbulence. Artificially reducing gyrokinetic collisions while strengthening gyrokinetic sources produces finer perpendicular structures. Linear analysis attributes the baseline turbulence to a drift-kinetic Kelvin--Helmholtz instability and identifies a gyrokinetic Kelvin--Helmholtz-like mode driven by short gyrokinetic density and potential gradient scales in the modified regime.

### Contributions

1. Performed coupled full-$f$ drift-kinetic and $\delta f$ gyrokinetic simulations in a linear device.
2. Derived the coupled electron, ion-gyromoment, and electrostatic equations from first principles.
3. Demonstrated rapid Hermite--Laguerre spectral convergence under physical LAPD collisionality.
4. Established that gyrokinetic fields are subdominant to drift-kinetic turbulence in that regime.
5. Identified a gyrokinetic Kelvin--Helmholtz-like mechanism for enhanced small-scale structure.
